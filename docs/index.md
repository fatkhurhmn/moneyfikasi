# Moneyfikasi

Moneyfikasi is a simple yet powerful tool to manage your personal or small business finances. Record transactions, categorize income and expenses, and analyze your habits through reports and graphs.

## Key Features

- **Recording Transactions.** Easily record income, expenses, and transfers between wallets.
- **AI Smart Input.** Add transactions by typing natural language sentences, powered by AI.
- **Financial Reports.** Detailed summaries of your financial activities over custom time periods.
- **Analysis Graphs.** Visualize spending and income trends with interactive charts.
- **Multiple Wallets.** Manage different accounts or cash sources in one place.
- **Customizable Categories.** Organize transactions with custom names, icons, and colors.
- **Budgeting.** Set and track budgets for different categories.
- **Recurring Transactions.** Automate regular payments and income tracking.
- **Backup & Restore.** Back up locally or optionally to your own Google Drive, restore anytime.
- **Export Data.** Export records to CSV or XLSX.
- **App Lock.** Protect your data with biometric authentication or PIN.

## Get the App

Moneyfikasi is available on Google Play. Search for "Moneyfikasi" on the Play Store.
