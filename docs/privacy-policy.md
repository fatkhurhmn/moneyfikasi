# Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Moneyfikasi is a personal finance app for tracking wallets, income, expenses, transfers, categories, budgets, presets, recurring transactions, statistics, and reports. The app is designed to keep your financial records on your device unless you choose to export or back them up.

## Data You Enter

Moneyfikasi stores the financial information you create, including wallet names and balances, categories, transaction amounts, notes, dates, budgets, presets, recurring transaction schedules, notification preferences, appearance settings, language settings, and app lock settings.

## Local Storage

Your app data is stored locally on your device. Moneyfikasi does not require an account, does not upload your financial records to a Moneyfikasi server, does not use ads or analytics SDKs, and does not sell your data.

## Google Drive Backup (Optional)

If you connect Google Drive, Moneyfikasi uses Google APIs to back up your data online:

- **What we access:** your Google account email and profile (for sign-in), and the app's own hidden folder in your Drive (`drive.appdata` scope, `appDataFolder`). The app can only read and manage files it created itself. It cannot see or access your other Drive files.
- **What we upload:** a ZIP file containing your local database (wallets, transactions, categories, budgets, and related records), named e.g. `moneyfikasi_yyyyMMdd_HHmmss.zip`.
- **How we use it:** solely to provide backup, restore, list, and delete features you trigger in Backup & Restore. We do not use Drive data for ads, analytics, or any other purpose.
- **Storage and sharing:** backup files are stored in your own Google Drive account under Google's privacy policy. We do not run our own server, and we do not share your Drive data with third parties, advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers.
- **Your control:** you can disconnect Google Drive anytime from the app (Logout), which signs you out locally. To remove online backups, delete them from the app's Drive list. Revoking access via your Google Account settings also stops further access.

Google user data access, use, storage, and sharing is limited to the practices disclosed in this policy.

## Backups and Exports

If you use local backup, restore, or export features, the app creates files such as backups, CSV files, XLSX files, or saved images in the location you select or authorize. Anyone with access to those files may be able to read the data inside them, so keep exported and backup files in a secure place.

## AI Features (Optional)

If you use AI Smart Input, the transaction text you type is sent to the configured AI provider (Groq / Google Generative AI) solely to parse it into a transaction. AI providers process it per their own privacy policies. We do not send your full financial history, only the text you submit.

## Permissions

Moneyfikasi may request notification permission to show local reminders and recurring transaction alerts. The app may also ask you to choose files or folders through Android system pickers for backup, restore, export, and image saving features. Internet access is used for AI parsing and optional Google Drive backup. Boot completed access is used to reschedule local recurring transaction reminders after the device restarts.

## Notifications

If you enable notifications, Moneyfikasi may create local notifications for recurring transactions and related reminders. Notification permission is controlled by your device settings and can be changed at any time.

## Security

App lock, PIN, and biometric options help protect access to the app on your device. Biometric verification is handled by Android system services. These features do not replace device-level security, encrypted backups, or careful handling of exported files.

## Retention and Deletion

Moneyfikasi keeps local app data until you edit it, delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Exported and backup files remain wherever you saved them until you delete those files yourself. Drive backups remain in your Google Drive `appDataFolder` until you delete them via the app.

## Third-Party Services

Moneyfikasi relies on Android system components and open source libraries to run app features, plus (only when you use them) Google Drive API and AI providers mentioned above. The app does not intentionally share your financial records with advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers.

## Your Control

You can add, edit, delete, export, back up, restore, or remove your data using the app features and Android storage controls. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data from Android settings may remove local records stored by the app.
